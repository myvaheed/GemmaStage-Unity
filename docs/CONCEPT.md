# 🚀 Project Title

**GemmaStage — Private VR Rehearsal for Ideas Before They Go Public**

---

# 🧠 Short Description

**GemmaStage is a local AI-powered VR rehearsal room where users test high-stakes ideas before exposing them to a real audience.**

Users step onto a virtual stage, present out loud, face AI-generated audience questions, and receive structured feedback on what was understood, what broke under questioning, and what still needs to be clarified — all fully on-device.

---

# 💡 Core Idea

Every important idea has a dangerous moment:

> the first time it leaves your head and has to make sense to someone else.

GemmaStage lets users rehearse that moment privately.

A startup pitch, research direction, thesis defense, technical proposal, product demo, or internal strategy may feel clear to the author, but fail when spoken aloud, questioned, or presented under pressure.

GemmaStage creates a private VR auditorium where users can:

* stand in front of a virtual audience
* explain their idea out loud
* use slides, diagrams, or board content
* face audience-style questions
* see what was actually understood
* improve before the real presentation

The system does **not** judge whether the idea is good.
It tests whether the idea survives explanation.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Most people prepare important presentations in isolation.

They write slides, rehearse mentally, or read a script — but they often do not experience the part that matters most:

> a room trying to understand them.

This creates real problems:

* ideas that feel clear internally become vague when spoken aloud
* speakers skip assumptions because they know too much about their own topic
* weak explanations only become visible when someone asks a question
* standard rehearsal does not recreate the pressure of being watched
* cloud-based AI tools are risky for unfinished or confidential ideas

For high-stakes ideas, the first real audience should not be the first real test.

---

# 💡 Solution

GemmaStage provides a **private VR rehearsal room with a local AI audience**.

The user enters a virtual auditorium and presents naturally. Gemma runs locally, listens to the presentation, tracks what an attentive audience would understand, raises questions when the explanation leaves gaps, and produces a structured post-session report.

GemmaStage helps answer:

1. **What did I actually communicate?**
2. **What did the audience understand?**
3. **Which questions would appear in the room?**
4. **Where did my explanation lose clarity, structure, or support?**
5. **Can I answer the questions my talk creates?**

This makes rehearsal active, not passive.

---

# 🥽 Why VR Matters

A presentation is not just content.
It is content under pressure.

Text-based rehearsal can help refine wording, but it does not train the experience of:

* standing on a stage
* speaking out loud
* seeing an audience in front of you
* managing pacing and presence
* being interrupted by questions
* recovering after uncertainty

VR turns rehearsal into embodied practice. The speaker does not just review an explanation — they perform it.

That matters because confidence is not built by reading notes.
It is built by repeated exposure to the situation.

---

# 🔒 Why Local-First Matters

GemmaStage is local-first because the most important ideas are often the least ready to share.

People need to rehearse:

* startup pitches before launch
* research ideas before publication
* product strategy before disclosure
* technical architecture before review
* thesis work before defense
* internal plans before they are public

These ideas are unfinished, sensitive, and sometimes valuable.

GemmaStage lets users test them without uploading their voice, slides, or reasoning to an external service.

> **Your idea can be tested before it is exposed.**

Local-first also provides:

* low latency
* offline use
* no external API dependency
* full control over private material

# 🤖 How Gemma 4 Is Used

Gemma 4 powers the local AI audience and evaluator.

It is used for:

## 1. Real-time perception

Gemma processes speech chunks and visual context such as slides, diagrams, or board content.

## 2. Audience-side recall

The system builds a running representation of what an ideal attentive audience member would take away from the talk.

This is not a transcript. It is the compressed understanding that the presentation successfully transferred.

## 3. Audience questions

Gemma tracks open concerns and turns them into audience-style questions.

These questions can appear during the talk or in a final Q&A phase.

## 4. Ground-truth comparison

When the user provides a private reference file, Gemma can compare what the user intended to communicate with what the AI audience actually understood.

This helps detect missing claims, weakened ideas, or unsupported explanations.

## 5. Structured feedback

Gemma produces feedback across a fixed rubric:

* main idea clarity
* structure
* consistency and focus
* support and justification
* language quality
* emotional delivery
* Q&A handling

All of this runs locally.

# 🎮 User Experience

## Mode 1 — Presentation Rehearsal

The user enters a VR auditorium and presents a high-stakes idea.

During the session:

* the user speaks naturally
* slides or visual material can be shown
* the AI audience tracks what is understood
* questions may appear when something remains unclear
* the user can answer live or continue the presentation

After the session:

* GemmaStage reconstructs what was communicated
* shows what the audience understood
* highlights gaps and unclear areas
* evaluates structure, support, language, delivery, and Q&A
* optionally compares the result against a private reference document

This is the launch focus.

---

## Mode 2 — Teach-to-Learn *(future feature)*

The same architecture can later support teaching-style rehearsal.

The user explains a topic as if teaching a class, and the system helps reveal gaps in their own understanding.

This is useful for:

* exam preparation
* lecture rehearsal
* technical study
* learning through explanation

Launch scope remains presentation rehearsal.
